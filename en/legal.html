In short: you're buying an eSIM with mobile internet abroad. The price in rubles is locked when you proceed to payment. Install the eSIM the day before your flight; the plan period starts from the first connection in the destination country. Until the eSIM is installed, we'll refund the full amount to your balance right away. After installation, refunds are by request only and at our discretion.

The Seller, Digitalpay FZE (details in section 10), offers any person with legal capacity to buy mobile data access abroad via eSIM on the terms below. Paying for an order means accepting this offer.

1. Definitions

1.1Service — the privetsim.net website, the Telegram bot and the “Privet SIM” Telegram Mini App.
1.2eSIM — a digital profile installed on a compatible phone via a QR code or activation code.
1.3Plan — a data allowance and validity period in a country or region. Either “for the whole period” or “with a daily limit”.
1.4Activation — the first connection of the eSIM to a network in the coverage country.
1.5Balance — the amount in rubles in your account. It consists of your own funds and bonuses.

2. Subject

2.1The Seller provides the Buyer with an eSIM with the selected plan, and the Buyer pays for it.
2.2The Seller is not a telecom operator. Connectivity is provided by local carriers through the eSIM supplier. Carriers and network type are listed on the country page.
2.3The eSIM provides mobile internet only. There is no phone number, calls or SMS.

3. Order and payment

3.1To place an order, the Buyer selects a plan and enters an email. After the first order, an account is automatically created for that email.
3.2The price is shown in rubles and may change with the exchange rate. The price shown when proceeding to payment is locked for that order.
3.3Payment methods: via SBP — the transfer is accepted on behalf of the Seller by the money transfer operator Murmansk Settlement Bank LLC (INN 5190103184); from the account balance (bonuses are used first); in USDT on TON, TRC-20 or BEP-20 (section 4). The transfer operator is not a party to the contract. The SBP payment link is valid for 30 minutes; after that, an unpaid payment is cancelled and the order must be placed again.
3.4An order is considered paid once the Seller receives payment confirmation from the transfer operator or from the blockchain. The payment confirmation is sent by email and kept in your account.
3.5Promo codes are valid under the terms of the specific promotion and cannot be exchanged for money. A promo code can be used once per email and once per card or wallet.

4. Cryptocurrency payment

4.1The USDT amount is calculated at the Service exchange rate when the invoice is created. The invoice is valid for 30 minutes.
4.2The Buyer transfers exactly the stated amount on the stated network and, for TON, with the stated comment. Network and exchange fees are paid by the Buyer.
4.3If more than the invoice amount arrives, the excess is credited to the balance. If less arrives, or after the invoice expires, the amount received is credited to the balance at the current Service rate.
4.4A transfer on another network, in another coin or without a comment may be lost. The Seller will help find it but does not guarantee a refund.

5. eSIM delivery and installation

5.1The QR code, manual installation details and instructions are sent by email and appear in your account, usually within a few minutes of payment. If the eSIM is not issued within 1 hour, the Seller issues it again or refunds the payment in full.
5.2Before paying, the Buyer checks that the phone supports eSIM and isn't carrier-locked. The list on the “Compatibility” page is for reference only.
5.3The eSIM must be installed within 180 days of purchase. After that, an uninstalled eSIM stops working.
5.4The QR code is single-use. Once installed, the eSIM is tied to the phone: it can't be moved to another device or restored after deletion.
5.5Until the eSIM is installed, the Buyer can send it to another person from the account.

6. Validity and top-ups

6.1The plan period runs from activation, or, for plans where this is stated on the plan page, from eSIM installation. A day is counted as 24 hours from activation, not by the calendar: an N-day plan ends exactly N × 24 hours after activation. The plan ends when the period expires or the data is used up, whichever comes first. Unused data expires at the end of the period.
6.2On plans with a daily limit, once the daily allowance is used up, the speed is reduced to the one stated in the plan description.
6.3While the plan is active, you can buy more data or days for the same eSIM if the plan supports it. After the period ends, the eSIM can't be topped up; you need a new one.
6.4Remaining data in the account is updated with a delay of up to 3 hours. The actual remaining data may be less than shown.

7. Balance

7.1The balance is topped up with USDT cryptocurrency. The amount in rubles is calculated at the Service rate shown on the top-up screen.
7.2The balance can be used to pay for eSIMs and top-ups in full or in part. The balance doesn't expire, can't be withdrawn and can't be transferred to other users.
7.3When an account is deleted, the remaining own funds can be refunded by request before deletion. Bonuses are canceled on deletion.

8.1The Seller is not responsible for the quality and coverage of local carriers' networks, restrictions on services imposed by the authorities of the country of stay (for example, messenger calls), or apps that restrict access from foreign IP addresses.
8.2The Seller is not responsible if the eSIM doesn't work because the Buyer deleted it, configured the phone incorrectly (for example, didn't turn on data roaming) or installed it on an incompatible device.
8.3The Seller's liability is limited to the cost of the disputed order.
8.4Using the eSIM for spam, fraud or resale without a contract with the Seller is prohibited. In case of violation, the Seller may suspend the eSIM without a refund.

9. Refunds and disputes

9.1Refunds are made by request only. Until the eSIM is installed and activated, the refund is full. After installation, the request is reviewed individually. See the Refund Policy for details.
9.2Disputes are resolved through negotiation via support. A claim can be sent to support@privetsim.net; the response time is 10 days. If no agreement is reached, the dispute is settled in court under the laws of the Russian Federation.
9.3The Seller may amend this offer. The new version applies to orders paid after its publication.
