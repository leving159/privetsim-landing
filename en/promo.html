FAQ

Do I need a lot of followers?

No, a personal account works too. Reach only affects the refund amount — up to 50% of the eSIM price.

How many posts can I send?

One per order. Bought an eSIM for a new trip? Post about it again and get another refund.

How will I know how much I get back?

We'll tell you the amount in our reply to your link, and the money will appear on your balance in your account and in the Telegram bot.

Why could a refund be declined?

Most often the post is private or deleted, doesn't mention privetsim.net or @PrivetSimBot, or the order was paid more than 30 days ago. Under the promotion terms we may also decline without giving a reason.

Where will you show my post?

We may feature the best reviews, photos and videos on the website, in the bot and on Privet SIM social media. If you don't want us to show your name or username, say so when you send the link.
