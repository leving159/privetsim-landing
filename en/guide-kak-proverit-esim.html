How to check if your phone supports eSIM

Before buying an eSIM, make sure of two things: your phone supports eSIM and it is not locked to a carrier. Both checks take a couple of minutes.

Updated October 25, 2026

Method 1. The *#06# code

Open the Phone app and dial *#06#. If the screen shows an EID line — a long 32-digit number — your phone supports eSIM. If only the IMEI is shown, there is most likely no eSIM.

Some Android phones do not show the EID even when they support eSIM — in that case check the settings.

Method 2. Phone settings

  • iPhone: Settings → Mobile Data. If you see “Add eSIM” or “Add Data Plan”, eSIM is supported.
  • Samsung: Settings → Connections → SIM manager. Look for “Add eSIM”.
  • Google Pixel: Settings → Network & internet → SIMs. Look for an option to download a SIM or add an eSIM.
  • Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO: Settings → SIM cards & mobile networks. If there is an “eSIM” or “Manage eSIM” section, it is supported.

Menu names depend on the system version, but eSIM is almost always next to the SIM card settings.

Method 3. The list of models

We have collected recent models on the Compatibility page: iPhone, Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, Honor and others. Find your model using the search.

For Samsung and Xiaomi, eSIM support depends on the version made for a specific country: models for Russia and China often come without eSIM. So also check the *#06# code or the settings.

iPhones made for mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau with two physical SIM slots do not support eSIM.

Is your phone locked to a carrier

Phones bought on contract from an operator may be locked to its SIM cards — such a phone will not work with someone else's eSIM. This is most common with phones from the USA, Japan and the UK.

On iPhone open Settings → General → About and find the carrier lock item: it should say there are no SIM restrictions. On Android the easiest way is to insert another operator's SIM card: if the phone accepts it, it is not locked.

Phones bought in Russia from regular stores are usually not locked to a carrier.

FAQ

I have an iPhone 11 — will it work?Yes. All iPhones from the XS and XR onwards support eSIM, except models for mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau with two physical SIM slots.
Does eSIM work on a tablet?Yes, iPads with cellular (Wi-Fi + Cellular) support eSIM.
My phone already has two SIM cards. Can I add an eSIM?Yes, if the phone supports eSIM: it is stored separately from physical SIM cards. Usually two lines can be active at once — turn off the one you do not need on the trip.
My phone does not support eSIM. What can I do?You can use a hotspot from a travel companion's phone with an eSIM installed, or buy a SIM card on arrival.
