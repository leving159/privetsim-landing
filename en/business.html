FAQ

What documents do you provide?

Contract, invoice, completion certificate and universal transfer document via e-document exchange or on paper. API and white label partners get a monthly sales report.

Can I issue eSIMs under my own brand?

Yes, travel agencies and integrators can use white label: emails, QR and account with your logo. Terms are shared after you submit a request.

How do bloggers get 20%?

From every order by followers who came via your link or promo code, 20% (up to 3,000 ₽ per follower) is credited straight to your Privet SIM balance. You can use it to pay for eSIMs for yourself and your loved ones; withdrawals aren't available yet. A separate dashboard with contract payouts will be available for API and white-label partners.

Is there an API?

Yes: a REST API to issue eSIMs, top up and check remaining data, plus status webhooks. Test access is provided after signing an NDA.
